## 01 Driving feel and fidelity

Do these before adding content; every later feature is judged through the driving.

- **Tyre temperature, wear and fuel load (M).** Per-wheel temperature from slip energy, cooling with speed; grip as a bell curve around an optimum (comfort ~70 °C, racing ~90 °C). Wear as accumulated slip work; fuel as draining mass. Show a four-tyre glyph and a fuel bar in the HUD. Unlocks pit stops.
- **Haptics and controller feel (S).** Gamepad dual-rumble from skid, curb surface, ABS/TCS activity and wall contact at 60 Hz; wheel centring from front lateral force where exposed.
- **Camera and game-feel pass (S).** FOV 55° to 72° with speed, spring-lagged chase camera with lateral-G roll, curb/impact shake, cockpit roll, bonnet camera with a visible wheel.
- **Barrier collisions like tyre walls (M).** Oriented box vs barrier line: contact normal and lever arm from the hitting corner, near-zero restitution, wall friction, angular impulse. Tyre walls absorb, Armco deflects.
- **Light damage (M).** Aero damage (less downforce, more drag), alignment pull, trimmed torque curve; decal scuffs; repair fee after the race. After barrier collisions.
- **Weather that evolves (M).** Wetness per centreline sample rising with rain and drying on the racing line; feeds tyre grip and the road texture. Spray, drops on the camera, forecast in race options.

## 02 Race craft, rules and AI

- **Track limits, cutting detection and penalties (M).** Four wheels beyond the curb for a distance invalidates the lap (time trial) or adds a time penalty (race); sector checkpoints prevent shortcuts; warning strip in the HUD; penalty summary on results.
- **Sector times, live delta and ghost car (S).** Three sectors from the corner list; delta bar vs best lap by arc length every 0.1 s; ghost from saved best-lap replay frames.
- **AI personalities and mistakes (M).** Traits (aggression, consistency, defensiveness) per driver; consistency adds noise to braking points; defensive lines under pressure, blue flags, rare lock-ups. Optional catch-up toggle, off by default.
- **Qualifying, pit stops and championships (L).** Short qualifying sets the grid; the pit lane in each track file becomes drivable with a limiter and a stop box; endurance events with mandatory stops; championships as event series with a points table. After tyre wear and fuel.

## 03 Career and content

- **Licence tests (M).** Four licences of eight tests (braking box, single corner, sector, lap) with gold/silver/bronze targets generated by the AI at three skill levels. A test is a race config plus start position, end condition and scoring rule.
- **Tuning depth (M).** Gear ratio editor with a speed-per-gear chart, LSD initial/accel/decel feeding the viscous term, camber and toe as static slip offsets, ride height changing CG and aero; tuning sheets per car and track.
- **Paint shop and livery editor (L).** Paint finishes as material presets, wheel styles from the rim generator, decals as a canvas texture over the paint UVs saved as a JSON layer list.
- **Used car market and economy (S).** Seeded weekly rotation of eight cars at 60 to 85 percent of list with mileage; depreciated selling; wishlist.
- **More circuits and layouts (S each).** Nürburgring GP, Bathurst, Road America, Watkins Glen, Zandvoort, Imola, Hockenheim, Le Mans (Bugatti); reverse and short layouts as data variants; one hand-built showcase track.

## 04 Presentation

- **Photo mode and replay director (M).** Director picks the camera seeing the most cars within 60 m, cuts on overtakes and lock-ups, holds three seconds minimum. Photo mode with free camera, exposure, depth of field, 2× PNG export.
- **Audio depth (M).** Transmission whine, turbo/blow-off per engine kind, tyre scrub by surface, gravel and curb rumble, wind above 150 km/h; music that ducks under the engine; PA and crowd bed by time of day.
- **Rendering upgrades (L).** Environment reflections on paint, SSAO, two-cascade shadows, instanced trees with two LODs; an automatic quality scaler on measured frame time.
- **Onboarding and accessibility (S).** First-run flow (choose input, first licence test, starter car), HUD control hints for three races, colour-blind palettes, UI scale slider.

## 05 Platform and robustness

- **Physics and AI in a worker (L).** SharedArrayBuffer double buffer; renderer interpolates between physics states; needs cross-origin isolation headers, falls back to the main thread.
- **Regression lap table in CI (S).** Extend the pace test into a car × track matrix (best lap, top speed, off-track count) failing on a 3 percent regression against a committed baseline; screenshot tests of every menu via the UI preview harness.
- **Save migrations, export and lap telemetry (S).** Versioned save with migration functions and JSON export/import; lap analysis screen with speed/throttle/brake traces vs best lap, CSV export.
- **Installable and mobile-aware (M).** Service worker precaching built assets and track JSON; manifest and icons; tilt or on-screen steering with auto throttle; mobile quality preset.
